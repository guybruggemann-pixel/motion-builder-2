## General craft
- Arrivals decelerate (out eases), departures accelerate (in eases); visible repositions use inOut. [motion-craft-research]
- Overshoot is a choice, not a default: editorial and corporate titles get none. [motion-craft-research]
- Text needs a readable hold: at least 1.5 s for a short title, longer for two lines. [motion-craft-research]
- Don't add an entrance to something that is already visible at frame 0 in the reference. [motion-craft-research]
- Repetition needs phase: use cloners and fields for repeats, not many unrelated keys. [motion-craft-research]
