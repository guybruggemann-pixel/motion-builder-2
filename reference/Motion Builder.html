Send to After Effects

  1. Click Export to After Effects and unzip the download.
  2. In AE, put the playhead where it should start. For an Animate in/out preset, select your layers (top to bottom = stagger order).
  3. File › Scripts › Run Script File and pick the .jsx.
  4. You get plain keyframes and normal effects. Nothing to install, nothing live, light to play back.
